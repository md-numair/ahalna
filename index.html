Mission

To effectively contribute to empowering, supporting, and rehabilitating those in need through high-quality programs and strategic community partnerships.

Vision

Excellence in caring for and rehabilitating those in need through self-sustaining resources and continuous community participation, in line with the highest

Strategic Goals

1- Contributing to development and providing aid among members of society
2- Contributing to providing humanitarian aid to needy families
3- Contributing to providing support to women and developing their capabilities and projects
4- Contributing to qualifying and training community groups through various training courses and workshops.
5- Contributing to addressing the issues of youth and university and higher institute graduates by raising the capabilities qualified to compete in the free labor market
6- Contributing to developing social studies that identify the most vulnerable and poor groups in society
7- Combating illiteracy and spreading education among society in cooperation with relevant authorities 8- Spreading the culture of love, affection and peaceful coexistence and consolidating the concept of community peace among community groups
9- Partnering with national and foreign organizations in providing aid
10- Contributing to instilling values and concepts that help in the continuity and sustainability of voluntary work among society
